## Overview

Restoration commitments are made at national and international scales, but they have to be verified tree by tree and landscape by landscape. This work combines high-resolution satellite imagery and AI with field measurements so that implementers, intermediary organizations, and funders can monitor, report, and verify (MRV) restoration credibly and efficiently.

## Land Restoration From Planting to Proof (2026)

A guidebook with a practical approach to MRV for local land restoration at scale. It was developed through several years of field testing and integrates practitioner expertise with advances in high-resolution satellite imagery and artificial intelligence.

- Guidebook: [World Resources Institute (2026)](https://doi.org/10.46830/wrigb.24.00067)

## African tree crown–stem allometry dataset (2026)

Satellite imagery can measure tree crowns but not trunk diameter, the main input to conventional biomass equations. This dataset provides the link: 3,493 individual trees with concurrent measurements of trunk diameter, crown diameter, height, and species, collected with a standardized protocol in AFR100 restoration landscapes in Kenya, Ghana, Rwanda, and Malawi.

- Preprint: [*Earth System Science Data Discussions* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-2026-756)
- Data: [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20862468)

## Automated quality control for training data

Vision-language models automate quality control across terabytes of training data, reducing labelling and review requirements by 90%.
