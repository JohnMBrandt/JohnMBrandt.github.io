An open data web portal for the Urban Environment and Social Inclusion Index (Data-Driven Yale, 2018). It used satellite data to map environmental and social equity at the neighborhood scale, so users could choose a city and compare indicators such as income, poverty, air quality, urban heat island intensity, and tree cover across its districts.
